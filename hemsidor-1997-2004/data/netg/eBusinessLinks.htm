Personalization

  • The definition of this concept from personalization.com:

    As used on the Internet, the term has come to mean "person-specific content." Personalized content may be advertising, items for sale, screen layout, menus, news articles, or anything else we see via the Internet. Personalization is the result of technology integrated into a website that allows the server to modify what is presented to each viewer. With personalization technology website simultaneously may see two completely different sets of information.

  • My own reflection is that we have different kinds of modification:

    • Personalisation = one-to-me
    • Customisation = one-to-one
    • Segmentation = one-to-all
    • General = one-to-anybody

    To customise information and services for each member in a target group, or segment of a target group. This is often called one-to-one. The manager of a web customised presence knows that they are communicating with a unique member in a target group, and it could also be detailed down to a specific role. This can be used to adjust provided information views and service packages.

    This could be compared to having a segmented web presence supporting a conversation characterised as one-to-all. On the other hand it could be compared to the more sophisticated level of adaptation. Personalisation that could be described as one-to-me.

    Both customisation and personalisation implies that users log in as a unique person. For personalisation users also have the possibility to update their own user preferences and configuration parameters. The users can adjust relevant information themselves.

    To segment information and services in order to address current target groups different needs and preferences. i.e. This could be called: one-to-all, at least the manager of a web presence knows that we are communicating with a group that has indicated an area of interests that can optimise web site structures, information views and service packages according to this. This could be compared to a non segmented, or general web presence, or having a conversation with one-to-anybody.

Some interesting examples, standards and products

  • MarketSite.net
    a business-to-business marketplace portal for electronic procurement providing commerce services and the ability to interoperate with numerous buying and selling applications.
  • HP e-speak
    is an open platform for the creation, composition, mediation, management, and access of Internet-based services. HP e-speak components are small software modules that perform basic e-service functions such as authentication and authorization, event monitoring, and more. E-services defined: An e-service is any asset that you make available via the Net to drive new revenue streams or create new efficiencies.
  • eCo Interoperability Framework Specification
    provides a single common protocol through which eCommerce systems can describe themselves, their services and their interoperability requirements.
