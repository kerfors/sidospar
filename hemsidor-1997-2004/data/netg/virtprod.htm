Abstract:

Current ways of accessing data require the user to have an overview of locations and structures of the databases, to know the associated database query languages or to be familiar with a pile of applications. A graphical view of the product could offer a more natural view accessing data from product databases. This paper describes Virtual Product as a way of accessing complex product and process data, summarizes new user interface technologies and highlights what real products of today actually means. The contributions in this paper are; a definition of the Virtual Product concept and a scenario describing the use of Virtual Product interfaces. The scenario is expanded with Advanced User Interface facilities. Characteristics of Real Products and product development in reality are also being exemplified in the scenario. The key question is: Virtual Product; An Advanced User Interface for Real Products?

Key words: 3D-Graphical User Interface, Virtual Product, Product Structure, CAD/PDM-integration
