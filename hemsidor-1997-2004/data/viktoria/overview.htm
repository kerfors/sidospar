Overview of new IT and IT use

Updated by Kerstin Forsberg 21 March 1999

One objective for our part time Resarch and study program at the Viktoria Institute for Research on Information Technology Use is to get an overview of what's happening; new IT and IT use. We try to read and discuss a lot, here are some of our sources and links and also some of my results during this work.

 

Sources

  • Communications of the ACM
  • Computers in Context - The Philosophy and Practice of System Design
    • See My Overview and personal reflections after reading this book.
  • BRINT
    A Business Researcher's Interests: Management & Technology Resource, a searchable knowledge map of Contemporary Business, Management and Information Technology issues.
  • Bitpipe.com
    A research database that specializes in locating in-depth technology research and analysis reports. In fact, bitpipe.com is the only resource available that provides a broad accounting of key technology analyst groups and their reports.
  • Actor Network Resource, An Annotated Bibliography (Version 1.1)
