Updated by Kerstin Forsberg 12 June 1999

Out of Control:
The New Biology of Machines, Social Systems and the Economic World
by Kevin Kelly

The Atom is the past.
The symbol of science for
the next centuryis the dynamical Net.

The Net is an emblem of multiplies.
Out of it comes swarm being - distributed being -
spreading the self over the entire web
so that no part can say, "I am the I".

What emerges from the collective
is not a series of critical individual actions
but a multitude of simultaneous actions
whose collective pattern is far more important.
This is the swarm model.

Knowledge, truth, and information flow
in networks and swarm systems.

There is no central keeper of knowledge in a network,
only curators of particular views.

What humans can't engineer, evolution can.

Despite the confusion about the word "evolution",
our strongest terms of change are rooted in
the organic: grow, develop, evolve, mutate,
learn, metamorphose, adapt.
Nature is the realm of ordered change.

Coevolution can be seen as two parties
snared in the web of mutual propaganda.
Coevolutionary relationships, from parasites
and allies, are in their sense informational.
A steady exchange of information welds
them into a single system.

Colearning might be a better term
for what coevolving creatures do.
Coteaching also work the participants
in coevolution are both learning and teaching
each other at the same time.

A company cannot be a learning company
without also being a teaching company.

The advantage no longer lies in "how you do a job"
but in "which job do you do?".

 

 

No more teams! Mastering the dynamics of Creative collaboration by Michael Schrage

Creative collaboration
- not the politicized realities of teams -
is the essential relationship for generating
effective innovation and ideas in organizations.

The Latin verb communicare -
doesn't mean "to communicate" but "to share".

The act of collaboration is an
act of shared creation and/or shared discovery.

Instead of asking
"How can we break down the complexity
into more manageable parts?"
we must ask ourselves
"What kind of relationships and interactions
can we create around this problem to solve it?"

Are you framing the problem/opportunity
in a way that incites and encourage collaboration?

The question no longer is
"What do I do with this?"
But "Who else should see this
so I can understand it better?"

Technology is really a medium for
creating productive environments.

 

 

Release 2.0 -
A design for living in the digital age
by Ester Dyson

Internet is more than a source of information;
it's a way for people to organize themselves.

A community is a shared asset,
created by the investment of its members.
The more you put in, the more you take.

As information flows faster and
as innovations are easier to copy and implement,
the ability to keep innovating consistently (or to acquire people who can) and time to market will provide
primary competitive advantage.

The Net erodes the separation
between the work and the person.

Employees are no longer judge only on the products they create or what they do inside the company,
but also on the way they interact with the world
around them on the Net.

As you develop or market your skills, there are four broad attributes that will be especially valuable:
Creativity, Performance personality,
Self marketing, and Emotional Intelligence

Creativity

Employees will be valued for what they can produce,
not for what they have produced.

Most successful will be those who can design innovations to help the company get or stay ahead.

Performance personality

People who can think quickly will prevail.
Can you respond quickly (rather than think slowly)?

In the age of the Net, there will be less time to think,
more need to quick response.

Self marketing

Companies will need to have real personality online -
which means persons.

In a world where competitive advantage comes
either from new design or from attention of people,
those who succeed will be those who are good
at getting their new design or themselves noticed.

Emotional Intelligence

As routine is sucked out of our daily lives,
people who can create stability from chaos will be key.

As change becomes constant,
leaders must have the flexibility and vision to handle it.
