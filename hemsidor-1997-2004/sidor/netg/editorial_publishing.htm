Editorial and Publishing Links

Updated by Kerstin Forsberg 5 May 1998

During the last couple of months I have been working with the development of an editorial system: Impress Prepage and Reporter. (The system is being implemented in two major newspapers Göteborgs-Posten and Svenska Dagbladet.)

Since I am a great believer in exploring and learning a new area using Internet as a key source I have collected a couple of links and resources related to this interesting work.

Of course this work is done mostly for myself. Comments or questions are welcome to my mailaddress (kerfor@netg.se).
