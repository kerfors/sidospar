Background

Born in July 1960, raised in Kungälv near Gothenburg on the west coast of Sweden. Living in Kungälv with my husband Björn and our son Erik.

Mechanical Engineer. Supplementary training in programming languages, application development process and methods, as well as project management.

Have been working at Volvo Data, Gothenburg since 1980, in several different positions and roles. Always focusing development--development of applications, methods, processes and market. Project manager for the Volvo common Internet-project, Volvo Web Wave, during 1996.

In the summer of 1997 I joined Astrakan Strategic Development as a consultant.

Professional Interests

Objectives

  • Finding new patterns and insights, not only introducing new technology.
  • Flow of ideas, not only flow of information.
  • Doing new things, not only old things in new ways, using Information Technology.
  • Use our tools, technical and otherwise, as tools for cultivating a process rather than designing a product. (From Computers in Context, by Dahlbom and Mathiassen)

Projects

I participate in a part time Research and study program at the Department of Informatics at University of Göteborg, part of the Viktoria Institute for Research on Information Technology Use. I see great opportunities to learn more about the future and present use of IT, in new types of organizations from a theoretical point of view.

Continuously follow the development and application of internet-technologies, with special focus on the emerging concept Extranet.

Since I am a great believer in exploring and learning a new area using Internet as a key source I have collected a couple of Editorial and Publishing Links related to my work with the development of our editorial system.

Use Creativity Tools and practice ideas from the Learning Organizations theories in both my studies and professional work.

Improve my English. See My English Companion.
