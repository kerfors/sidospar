Research and study program

Updated by Kerstin Forsberg 2 February 1999

This is my study-page for the part time research and study program that I joined in March 1997 at Department of Informatics at University of Göteborg, part of the Viktoria Institute for Research on Information Technology Use.

Research

During the last months I have tried to focus my research around IT use in the media branch: media products such as newspapers are interesting artifacts and I find the editorial process and newsroom fascinating. I have collected a couple of Editorial and Publishing Links.

Inspired by concepts and ideas from our CSCW course I am trying to do some fieldwork studying cooperation in the editorial process, together with my supervisor: Fredrik Ljungberg, focusing cooperation and mobility in a newsroom. The study reported in our paper The Organizing of Editorial Work: Eliciting Implications for New IT use have initiated a forthcoming investigation in detail of day to day accomplishment of newspapers as well as other media such as web sites and radio programs with news content. This investigation, called MobiNews is part of the program: Mobile Informatics, at the Viktoria Research Institute.

Courses

I see great opportunities to learn more about the future and present use of IT, in new types of organizations from a theoretical point of view. During our study program we try to establish an overview of new IT and IT use. Our study program includes following courses:

  • Human Computer Interaction
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Computer-Supported Cooperative Work
  • IT and Management
  • Software Engineering
  • …..

My ambition is to use the courses, important books and valuable articles as "launch-pads". Identifying what key concepts, interesting persons and thought-provoking ideas I would like to use as starting points for my studies and thesis. Linking to other interesting resources, use them as perspectives to reflect on my profession, and to how apply them in my everyday work.
