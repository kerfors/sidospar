A Virtual Product Scenario

Part of the thesis
The Virtual Product; An Advanced User Interface for Real Products?

A 'green engineering' scenario. The scenario includes a couple of scenes from the development and marketing of light-weight products and recycling components. The scenario is expanded with Advanced User Interface facilities. Characteristics of Real Products and product development in reality are also being exemplified in the scenario.
Scen one - Product Development

The Virtual Product is used as a 'through-the-window' tool for the product manager to highlight components on a 3D representation of a complete new version of the product. Components that are above their specified weight, but only components that have a total development cost for all assembled parts below planned cost for the whole component.

For the development team working with a specific release of the component, the Virtual Product interface is being used to select parts using a standard mouse. Parts that are often shared with other products. Expand all weight and material properties from different databases. Click on the link to the supplier of a single part and directly set up a online-conference with the supplier discussing change proposal to decrease the weight. Simulate and visualize the effect of the proposed changes on the specific revision of the part on the 3D representation of both on the assembled component and on the complete product for all team-members in real-time at their different locations.

Scen two - Process Development for the Product

A Virtual Product environment that allows virtually disassemble a specified release of a component, including activities such as accessing and removing parts and fasteners. Using a Glove Input Device to manipulate virtual tools to determine these disassembly sequence and removal paths.

Scen three - Product Marketing

Change configuration for a product, but not outside the limitations for the specific market and environmental laws and regulations, together with a customer using standard menus. Simulate usage of a 3D representation of the configurated product in different virtual environments. Produce a simulated emission graph, a document summarizing the environmental effect together with a 3D drawing of the configurated product, price and date of delivery.
