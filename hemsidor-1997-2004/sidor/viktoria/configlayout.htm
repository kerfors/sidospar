to Configuration

Abstract:

Newspapers and newspaper production have a rich tradition and a body of knowledge how to attract readership and to optimize the effectiveness in presenting information. Placement of articles on pages -- the configuration of the news items in a container -- is one of the key activities in newspaper production. The traditional daily newspaper has become one of several media for news publishing -- a suite of information products -- each type having it's own implicit and explicit layout rules and constraints. This paper describes techniques and theories from the Artificial Intelligence area applicable for a automatic, or knowledge-based, layout tool: CIP - Configurator for Information Products.

Key words: Newspaper layout, Automatic layout, Automatic pagination,
Artificial Intelligence, Media-independent publishing
