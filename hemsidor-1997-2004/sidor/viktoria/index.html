Background

Born in July 1960, raised in Kungälv near Gothenburg on the west coast of Sweden. Living in Kungälv with my husband Björn and our son Erik.

Have been working at Volvo Data, Gothenburg from 1980 until 1997, in different positions and roles. All of them focusing development - development of applications, methods, processes and market. Project manager for the Volvo common Internet-project, Volvo Web Wave.

In the summer of 1997 I joined Astrakan Strategic Development as a consultant. In January 1999 Astrakan become part of Adera.

I was one of the initiators of a company: Instant Context Technologies Sweden AB (ICTech). Our product FieldWise was a mobile context manager for the mobile professional customer. It was one of the results from the research project that I participatde in: MobiNews, part of the Mobile Informatics program at the Viktoria institute in Gothenburg. (Research and study program).

Since September 2001 I am employed at AstraZeneca and become part of the R&D IS organisation: Information Architecture in Mölndal, south of Gothenburg. Recently, I have got the opportunity to work as a Senior Informatics Architect within in the Medical Informatics organisation part of Clinical Science.

 

 

Professional Interests

Most of my assignments seem to have something to do with these topics:

  • Information architecture and structuring
  • Content management systems
  • Editorial processes
  • Ontologies and taxonomies
  • XML with a special interest in metadata applications (See also my overview on the topic of: What is metadata?) and metadata standards, such as
    • RDF
    • Dublin Core
    • Topic Maps
              

Since 1998 I combine these interests with a great curiosity for emerging mobile services and applications. At Adera I participated in the Excellence Centre for Mobile Business Services and I also had a position as Trend Watcher. My Ph.Lic thesis (Mobile Newsmaking) combined my interests in information architecture and structuring with mobile services for journalist, a professional group highly mobile.

In the R&D organisation at AstraZeneca I work with information architecture related questions in domains such as clinical data and documents to enhance the development of drugs through the use of corporate portals and information retrieval technologies and information integration by applying Data Warehouse and ETL (Extract Transfer Load) tools.

Objectives

  • Finding new patterns and insights, not only introducing new technology.
  • Flow of ideas and knowledge, not only flow of data and documents.
  • Doing new things, not only old things in new ways, using Information Technology.
  • “Use our tools, technical and otherwise, as tools for cultivating a process rather than designing a product.” (From Computers in Context, by Dahlbom and Mathiassen)

Projects

I have participated in a part time Research and study program at the Viktoria Institute for Research on Information Technology Use. This did result in a Ph.Lic thesis (Mobile Newsmaking) in December 2001.

I am currently a member of Biznet, a network between Volvo, SKF and AstraZeneca together with the IT University of Göteborg.

Between March 1997 and November 1999 I did continuously follow the development and application of internet-technologies, with special focus on the emerging concept Extranet.
