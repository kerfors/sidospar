Master thesis projects

Updated by Kerstin Forsberg 26 July 2003

If you are looking for a project for your Bachelor or Master thesis (or what we in Sweden call an "Exjobb") you have come to the right place. At AstraZeneca there is always a opportunity to do something interestingly.  Please contact me to hear the details or discuss your own creative ideas.

Below is a list of both previous and current projects!

Publications

  • Using the document management system GEL from an exploitation perspective  –  A case study at AstraZeneca, by Maria Adolfsson Joakim Bengtsson at the IT University of Göteborg (only an AstraZeneca internal thesis available).

  • Learning and knowledge sharing in a networking environment, by Per Ahlström, Martin Berg & Adam Winberg at the IT University of Göteborg.

  • An evaluation of Topic Maps, by Anna Carlstedt and Mats Nordborg Johansson at Computational Linguistics, Göteborg University.

  • An Investigation of Methods for Improving a Mobile Knowledge System, Resulting in the Named Entity Recognizer NEX, by Johannna Bohman and David Johansson at Computational Linguistics, Göteborg University. Done in collaboration with my previous company Instant Context Technologies.
